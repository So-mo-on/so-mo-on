Symptom Network

This interactive visualization represents relationships between diseases based on shared symptoms. Each node corresponds to a disease, and two diseases are connected when they have one or more symptoms in common. The strength of each connection reflects the degree of similarity, meaning how many symptoms are shared between them.

To explore the network, search for a disease using the input field. The visualization will highlight its connections, allowing you to see how it relates to other conditions based on symptom overlap.

This visualization is intended for exploratory and educational purposes. It highlights patterns of symptom similarity and does not represent clinical relationships or diagnoses. For medical concerns, consulting a qualified healthcare professional is recommended.
